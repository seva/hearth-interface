# Hearth PR/FAQ Document

This document contains the official press release and internal Frequently Asked Questions for Hearth, a platform for fractional co-ownership of short-term rentals.

---

## Press Release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Hearth Unlocks Vacation Home Ownership, Allowing Anyone to Invest in and Enjoy a Luxury Property for as Little as $100**

**SEATTLE – July 14, 2027** – Hearth, a new technology platform, launched today to make equity ownership in vacation homes a tangible reality for everyone. For the first time, individuals can browse a curated collection of high-performing short-term rentals, purchase a fractional share for as little as $100, and earn passive income while also having access to enjoy the property themselves.

Hearth is creating a new asset class that sits at the intersection of lifestyle and investment. The platform handles the entire lifecycle of co-ownership, from vetting and acquiring properties to forming a property-specific LLC for the co-owners. Post-acquisition, Hearth’s integrated technology manages all aspects of the short-term rental operation, from dynamic pricing to guest communication and maintenance, with the goal of maximizing returns for its owners.

"True ownership should be an engine for wealth creation, not a liability," said the CEO of Hearth. "We've built a transparent platform that turns a luxury good into an accessible investment. Our owners get the financial benefits of a high-performing asset and the personal benefit of a vacation home they can create memories in."

All co-owners have access to a real-time dashboard showing property performance, and profits are distributed automatically. A fair, credit-based system ensures all co-owners have an equitable opportunity to book stays at their property for only the cost of cleaning.

Hearth is launching with a collection of properties in Florida and Arizona. Sign up for early access and browse the first collection of properties at [www.joinhearth.com](http://www.joinhearth.com).

## Internal FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: How is this different from a timeshare?**

**A:** Hearth is a new model of co-ownership where you buy **real equity** in a property, not just the "right to use" it. A timeshare is a pre-paid vacation expense that depreciates in value. A Hearth property is an investment asset that can appreciate and generate rental income. We provide a path to liquidity for your equity, full financial transparency, and a flexible owner-stay system—all things the old timeshare model lacks.

**Q: What are the risks?**

**A:** We believe in transparency. Any investment carries risk, and ours has three primary types:
1.  **Market Risk:** The value of real estate can go down as well as up.
2.  **Performance Risk:** Rental income is not guaranteed and can fluctuate based on seasonality, demand, and other factors.
3.  **Liquidity Risk:** Your ownership stake is not a publicly traded stock. At launch, there is no secondary market, so you should view this as a medium-to-long-term investment. We plan to build a secondary marketplace to provide a path to liquidity in the future.

**Q: What are the fees?**

**A:** Our fee structure is simple and transparent.
1.  **Sourcing Fee:** A one-time fee (e.g., 3% of the property's price) is included in the initial offering to cover the costs of acquiring and preparing the property.
2.  **Management Fee:** We charge a recurring management fee (e.g., 15% of gross rental revenue) to cover the professional management, marketing, and operation of the property. There are no hidden fees.

**Q: How do I make money?**

**A:** You earn money in two ways: through passive income from rental revenue, distributed quarterly, and through potential long-term appreciation of the property's value.

**Q: Why should I trust Hearth to manage the property?**

**A:** Our advantage is in our **fully integrated technology platform.** While other solutions require stitching together separate tools for booking, maintenance, and finance, our system unifies them. This gives us—and our owners—a single, real-time view of the asset's health. This total integration allows us to operate properties with greater efficiency and transparency, which we believe leads to better returns. Our interests are aligned with yours: we only succeed if your property succeeds.

**Q: What is Hearth's role vs. the owners' role?**

**A:** Hearth acts as the **platform facilitator and asset manager** on behalf of the co-owned LLC. Our role is to maximize the property's financial performance and handle all operational complexities. The owners' role is to contribute capital and participate in major governance decisions. While owners can enjoy the property, all use is subject to the rules of a fair, standardized "Co-ownership Agreement" that every investor signs. This agreement is designed to protect the value of the asset for all co-owners.

**Q: How are decisions made?**

**A:** Day-to-day operational decisions (e.g., pricing, marketing, minor repairs) are handled by Hearth's management team to ensure professional and efficient operation. Major decisions that impact the core ownership of the asset (e.g., selling the property, taking on significant debt) require a majority vote from the co-owners, weighted by ownership percentage. All voting is conducted formally and transparently through the Hearth platform.

**Q: What happens if an owner or their guest causes a problem?**

**A:** The Co-ownership Agreement that all owners sign clearly outlines the rules for personal use, guest policies, and liability for damages. This ensures that all co-owners are treated fairly and the property is protected. Our management team handles the enforcement of these rules on behalf of the LLC.
