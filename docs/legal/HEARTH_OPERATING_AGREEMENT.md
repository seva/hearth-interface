3.1 **Tokens as Membership.** Membership in the DAO is represented by holding the [Hearth Governance Token] (Symbol: [HRTH]). 
3.2 **Voting.** All managerial decisions, including treasury allocations, asset acquisitions, and protocol upgrades, shall be determined by a token-weighted vote of the Members via the Governance Contract.
3.3 **Quorum.** A minimum quorum of [4%] of circulating supply is required for any proposal to execute.

## ARTICLE IV: LIABILITY
4.1 **Liability Shield.** Debts options, obligations, and liabilities of the Company, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be solely the debts, obligations, and liabilities of the Company. No Member or Manager (Smart Contract) shall be obligated personally for any such debt, obligation, or liability of the Company solely by reason of being a Member or acting as a Manager.

## ARTICLE V: DISSOLUTION
5.1 **Events.** The Company shall dissolve upon:
    (a) A proposal passed by Members via Smart Contract to dissolve;
    (b) The permanent failure of the Smart Contract system;
    (c) Judicial decree.
