# Hearth Protocol: Legal Setup (Wyoming DAO LLC)

**Goal:** Establish the legal entity for the Hearth Protocol.
**Structure:** Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous Organization LLC.
**Cost:** ~$100 Filing Fee + ~$150/yr Registered Agent.

## 1. Preparation
*   **Name Search:** Verify "Hearth Protocol DAO LLC" or similar is available on [Wyoming SOS](https://sos.wyo.gov).
*   **Registered Agent (RA):** You need a physical address in Wyoming. Use a service like:
    *   *Northwest Registered Agent* (~$125/yr)
    *   *Wyoming Registered Agent* (~$50/yr)
    *   **Action:** Sign up for an RA service online first. They will provide the address you need for the filing.

## 2. Filing the Articles of Organization
*   **Portal:** Wyoming Secretary of State Business Center.
*   **Form:** "Articles of Organization for a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO)".
*   **Key Fields:**
    *   **Name:** Must include "DAO LLC", "LAO", or "DAO".
    *   **Management:** Select **"Algorithmically Managed"**. (This is critical for our whitepaper narrative).
    *   **Smart Contract Address:** (Optional at filing, but good to have a placeholder or "TBD").
    *   **Registered Agent:** Enter the info from Step 1.
*   **Fee:** $100 (plus ~$4 online fee).

## 3. Post-Filing
*   **EIN:** Apply for an EIN from the IRS online (Free). Use "Hearth Protocol DAO LLC".
*   **Operating Agreement:** Wyoming law requires a "DAO Operating Agreement".
    *   *Note:* The Smart Contract *can* serve as the Operating Agreement, but a text-based wrapper referencing the contract address is safer for banking.

## 4. Banking (The Hard Part)
*   **Crypto-Native:** **Mercury** or **Relay** *might* accept a DAO LLC if you have an EIN and human signer.
*   **Fallback:** **Kraken Institutional** or similar crypto-friendly rails for holding the USDC yield.
