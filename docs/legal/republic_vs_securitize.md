## Deep Dive Analysis

### 1. Fee Structure & Economics
*   **Republic:** Their model is "we win if you win." For a $500k raise, paying ~7% ($35k) *after* you close is far superior to paying ~$25k *before* you open. The sheer cost of Securitize's implementation fee + legal technical setup makes it non-viable for raises under $1M-$2M.
*   **Securitize:** Starts making sense at raising $3M+. Below that, their fixed costs are a high burn rate.

### 2. Secondary Market & Liquidity
*   **Securitize:** Is the clear leader in trading technology. If day-one liquidity and continuous trading are your *primary* goals, Securitize is the only real option.
*   **Republic:** Historically relied on "periodic liquidity events" (not true trading). However, with the **acquisition of INX Digital (April 2025)**, they are integrating a true ATS. For a 2026 launch, this distinction is less critical than it was in 2024.

### 3. Marketing & Distribution
*   **Republic:** You are paying for their **email list**. They will blast your deal to thousands of verified accredited investors who are actively looking for real estate deals.
*   **Securitize:** They are a **technology provider**, not a marketing agency. You must bring your own investors. They provide the "plumbing" to tokenize them, but they won't fill your round.

## Recommendation: Go with Republic

**Republic is the correct choice for Hearth DAO's $500k target.**

1.  **Cash Flow Friendly:** No massive upfront check to write.
2.  **Marketing Included:** You need help *filling* the $500k; Republic's audience can do that. Securitize assumes you already have the money lined up.
3.  **Future-Proof:** With the INX acquisition, you will likely have a path to secondary trading by the time your 1-year Reg D lockup expires.
