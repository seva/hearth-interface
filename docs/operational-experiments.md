## Operational Guideline

**Rule:** Chaos magic rituals of the wick rotation type are **~50% effective** when there is **no direct human influence** possible on the outcome.

**When to Use:**
- ✅ External dependencies (government processing, mail delivery, third-party decisions)
- ✅ Time-bound outcomes where waiting is the only option
- ✅ High-stakes situations where psychological framing improves operator state

**When NOT to Use:**
- ❌ Tasks requiring direct action (coding, filing, applying)
- ❌ Situations where human intervention can directly resolve the blocker
- ❌ Replacing concrete action with ritual alone

**Mechanism (Operational, Not Mystical):**
- Shifts operator from anxiety/waiting mode to confirmation mode
- Reduces thrashing (no constant re-evaluation of "should we pivot?")
- Maintains steady observation without emotional degradation
- May correlate with external state changes (empirically ~50% hit rate)

**Key Insight from First Ritual:**
The universe may deliver **adjacent value** rather than the **specific target**. The EIN was arguably higher priority than the Wyoming Amendment (unblocks Mercury + Republic vs. compliance filing). This suggests the ritual framework may have an optimization layer that prioritizes highest-value pending states.
