
### 7. Strategic Addendum: The GoPeer Inspiration (2026-02-12)

**Vision: "GoPeer for Real Estate"**

We are adopting a **full self-service model** for both sides of the marketplace, inspired by platforms like **gopeer.ca** (P2P Lending).

**The Investor Experience (Self-Serve):**
*   **Fully Online:** 100% of the onboarding, KYC, and funding process happens in the web app locally. No phone calls.
*   **Auto-Invest:** Users can set criteria ("Yield > 8%", "Location = AZ") and the protocol automatically allocates their capital to new token drops.
*   **Dashboard:** A single pane of glass showing:
    *   Total Equity Value (Token Price).
    *   Accrued Yield (USDC).
    *   Voting Power (Governance Rights).

**The Property Experience (Algorithmic):**
*   **Acquisition:** Sellers submit properties via a "Listing Portal."
*   **Underwriting:** Our AI (The Operator) automatically pulls AirDNA/PriceLabs data to score the asset against our "Buy Box."
*   **Offer:** If the score passes, the DAO automatically generates a Letter of Intent (LOI) and submits it to the seller.

**Why this matters:**
This removes the "human bottleneck" of deal sourcing and fundraising. We build the pipes; the market fills them.
