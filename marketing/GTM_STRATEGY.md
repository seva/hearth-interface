# Hearth DAO - Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. Narrative: "The Landlord is Dead. Long Live the Code."
*Positioning Hearth not as a REIT, but as the first "Headless Real Estate Brand".*

**Core Pillars:**
-   **Zero Friction:** No board meetings, no paper leases (for investors).
-   **Zero Trust:** Treasury is a Timelock. Ops are proven on-chain.
-   **Maximum Yield:** Removing the 30% Property Manager fee increases cap rates significantly.

## 2. Target Audience: "The Crypto-Native Rentier"
*We are NOT targeting traditional real estate boomers (yet).*

**Ideal Profile:**
-   Holdings: $10k-$100k in USDC/ETH on Base.
-   Pain Point: Yield farming is risky/volatile. Wants "Real World Assets" (RWA) but hates KYC/Paperwork.
-   Platforms: Farcaster (Warpcast), Twitter (Crypto Twitter), Base Discord.

## 3. Channels & Outreach

### B. Twitter (Secondary - Broadcast)

## 4. Launch Campaign: "The Genesis Block"
*Goal: Raise $500k USDC for Property #1.*

**Phase 1: The Signal (Weeks 1-2)**
-   Publish Whitepaper v1 (Done).
-   Verify Contracts (Done).

**Phase 2: The Whitelist (Weeks 3-4)**
